Performance Thresholds

W

Your maximum sustainable power output for ~1 hour (watts). Used to compute cycling TSS. Estimate via a 20-minute all-out effort × 0.95.

bpm

Heart rate at lactate threshold (bpm). Used when power data is unavailable. Estimate as ~88–92% of max HR, or from a race/hard effort.

bpm

Your maximum heart rate (bpm). Used to define training zones. Estimate as 220 − age, or from a maximal-effort test.

sec/km

Running pace you can sustain for ~1 hour, in seconds per kilometre (e.g. 240 = 4:00/km, 270 = 4:30/km). Used to compute running TSS.

– bpm

Heart-rate band for Zone 2 aerobic work. Used to calculate Zone 2 minutes per workout.

Changing these will recompute TSS for new workouts only. Past workouts keep their original TSS values (no retro recompute in this sprint). Zone 2 weekly minutes are tracked as a habit — edit the target on Habits.
