How it works
  • Type sets the run's run_subtype: Easy, Long, Tempo, Intervals, Race. One click saves; click the selected one again to clear.
  • Dashed with a green dot = the type the app guessed from the run. It stays a guess until you click it.
  • Felt keeps today's Hard / OK / Easy. The row label stops "Easy" (type) and "Easy" (felt) from reading as the same thing.
  • Strength and other sessions show a fixed type tag and only the Felt row.
  • Selected colours match the calendar and the volume chart: Race is the dark lime chip, same as the RACE bar.
  • At 390 px both rows sit under the title, full width, and the buttons stay 30 px tall.
